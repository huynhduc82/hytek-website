ABOUT HYTEK

We understand the equipment behind the software.

HYTEK SOLUTIONS VN COMPANY LIMITED is a Vietnam-based software engineering company specializing in semiconductor and advanced thin-film equipment.

Our engineering experience in this field dates back to 2003. We have developed and supported software across process chambers, wafer handling systems, loadlocks and integrated multi-chamber platforms.

EQUIPMENT SIMULATION

Equipment simulators for development, testing and training.

Alongside equipment software, HYTEK builds simulators that stand in for real hardware — process chambers, wafer handling, devices and the factory host — so software can be developed, tested and demonstrated without waiting for the tool.

Virtual equipment

Chambers, loadlocks, robots and process hardware modelled with realistic timing, states and responses.

Device simulators

MFCs, RF/DC power supplies, pumps, valves and controllers answering over the same protocols as the real devices.

I/O and interlock simulation

Digital and analog signals, sensors and interlocks, so sequences and safety logic can be exercised at a desk.

Fault injection

Alarms, timeouts and hardware failures on demand, to test error handling and recovery before they happen on the tool.

Host simulation

GEM/SECS host scenarios for testing factory integration without a production MES.

Training and demonstration

Operators and customers can run the equipment software end to end without a cleanroom.
